# Kukoon growth tracker

A single self-contained page (`index.html`) covering:

- **Health check**: revenue, new customers, MER and ad spend against the internal targets, plus ranked findings.
- **Site**: Shopify sessions, conversion rate, AOV, checkout funnel, traffic sources, device and country.
- **New customers**: an overview, plus the detailed Google new-customer reports (visitors, customers, CAC and ROAS, campaign matrix, Northbeam against Shopify, Shopify channels).
- **Channels**: Northbeam across every channel (the source of truth for spend, revenue, ROAS, new customers, CPA and new visits), with Meta and Google Ads in-platform views.
- **Black Friday**: the 2026 plan by phase and day against last year, and the commercial calendar.
- **Actions**: Meta and Google proposals waiting for a go-ahead.

Data is embedded in the page and refreshed on demand. This version has data to 28 October 2026.
